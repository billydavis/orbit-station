Analog clock faces

Ideas for the analogClock control, drawn at the real 240×240 size with the firmware's Roboto and the colours cut to RGB565 like the board. The pictures are generated stand-ins; on the board a background would be your own uploaded picture or a Makapix artwork. Pick a face to load it into the builder below.

Drawing it on the board

  • Smooth hands: LovyanGFX 1.3 already has drawWedgeLine (tapered, anti-aliased, round ends), drawSmoothLine and fillSmoothCircle. Dauphine and sword shapes are filled triangles, whose edges come out a little harder than in this mockup.
  • Cached layer: the background, dial and numerals change only when a setting does, so they go in a PSRAM layer like claudeUsage's. A frame then copies the layer and draws three hands: an estimated 2–4 ms.
  • Sweep: about 10 frames a second instead of 1. Only the tiles under the second hand change, so the bus barely notices, but core 0 draws it 10 times as often.

Pictures and saving

  • Your own picture: a 240×240 JPEG posted to /orbit/api/v1/screens/{n}/background, kept in LittleFS (/orbit-bg{n}.jpg, typically 15–40 KB) and written only when it changes. It never goes in NVS.
  • Makapix art: one artwork from a channel or post id, decoded by the existing Makapix code. It's a still behind the hands, and an animated one could play.
  • Everything is a setting: nothing here is live data, so stripLive() has nothing to remove. The time zone for the second hand would be an IANA name, like /api/v1/settings.
